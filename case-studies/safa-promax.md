## Overview

SAFA ProMax is a board-facing creative-technology platform designed to bring strategy presentation, stakeholder communication, structured content and digital delivery into one coherent experience.

The protected live artefact demonstrates the approved public-facing experience. This case study explains the architecture, delivery judgement and governance boundary without publishing the working implementation repository.

## The Challenge

Strategic and stakeholder material can become fragmented across presentations, documents, email threads and disconnected review processes. The challenge was to shape that material into a clear digital experience that supports executive review while preserving a strict boundary around protected operations, records and internal implementation details.

## My Role

I shaped the platform architecture, full-stack delivery direction, information flow, responsive experience, visual system and governance approach. The work demonstrates how business strategy can be translated into a reviewable digital platform without publishing confidential implementation material.

My bounded contribution covers:

- systems architecture and implementation direction;
- responsive web-platform and interface delivery;
- creative-technology and visual-system direction;
- stakeholder presentation flow and board-readiness thinking;
- structured content and review-workflow design;
- governance, documentation, verification and handover controls.
